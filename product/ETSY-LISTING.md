## 5. Listing settings checklist

- **Test first (important):** I verified every formula by recalculating in LibreOffice, but I could not open the file in Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets from here. Before you publish, open it in both (and the Sheets phone app if you can), type a few made-up numbers, and confirm the Today number changes. The "Works with" lines in the description depend on this.
- **Type:** Digital (instant download). Upload `Calm-Budget-Planner.xlsx`.
- **Who made it:** I did. **What is it:** a finished product.
- **Generative AI field:** answer honestly. The images are screenshots, but the product was built with AI assistance. Etsy's current rules and wording change, so read their Creativity Standards page when you list.
- **Photos:** upload `listing-image-1.png` first, then 2 to 5 in order.
- **Category:** Craft Supplies & Tools or Paper & Party Supplies > Templates (Etsy shows the exact path in the category picker; choose the closest "Templates / Planners" option).
- **Shipping:** none needed for digital.

## 6. Keyword research: what I found and what I couldn't

**Found**
- Budget spreadsheets are proven sellers (listings with 12k+ sales), and ADHD planners are trending up on Etsy in 2026.
- The top ADHD life-planner spreadsheet has 57k+ sales and a 4.7★ rating, so the niche has real demand.
- Competitor titles lead with "ADHD Budget Planner / Spreadsheet" plus "Google Sheets". That is the crowded term. We use it, but it is not where we stand out.
- "Safe to spend" is already used by at least two "ADHD Budget Planner, Safe to Spend Tracker" listings (~$10–11), described as offline apps rather than Excel / Google Sheets files. So it proves demand, but **it isn't unclaimed**. Don't claim to be the first.
- Impulse-spending trackers and subscription trackers exist as separate products; I didn't find them combined with a daily safe-to-spend number in one editable spreadsheet.
- Etsy now favours natural phrases and puts weight on the start of the title. 2–4 word buyer phrases work best.

**How we stand out (the real edge)**
1. The bundle: safe-to-spend + impulse wait list + subscription check in one file.
2. Works in **both** Excel and Google Sheets (many competitor titles say Google Sheets only; I saw this in titles, not every listing).
3. Auto-rolls each month and uses one date-paid cell, so there's no monthly reset ritual.
4. Kind, no-shame design and wording.

**Keyword tiers**

| Tier | Keywords | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Proven demand (crowded) | adhd budget planner, budget spreadsheet, google sheets budget | Needed to be found at all |
| Our differentiators | safe to spend, daily spending limit, adhd budget excel, impulse spending, subscription tracker | Fewer budget listings combine these with a spreadsheet |
| Supporting | paycheck budget, bill tracker, monthly budget, budget template, neurodivergent | Broad reach |

**What I could NOT do**
- Etsy blocks my access, so I couldn't read competitors' hidden tags or see real search-volume numbers.
- Before you publish, check the volume and competition for each tag in **eRank** (free tier) or **Marmalead**, or in Etsy's own Shop Manager search stats once you have sales. Swap out any tag that turns out to have almost no searches.
- Ideas to test as replacements: "zero based budget", "biweekly budget", "no spend tracker", "budget for irregular income" (the last one would need a small workbook change; ask me).

## 7. Honest expectations

- You'll have no reviews on day one, and the top listings have thousands. Expect slow starts. Sales depend heavily on your first photo, your title, and early reviews.
- I can't promise any number of sales.
- Etsy costs per sale: $0.20 listing fee + 6.5% transaction + about 3% + $0.25 payment processing. On a $9.99 sale that's about $1.40 in fees, so you keep about $8.59.
