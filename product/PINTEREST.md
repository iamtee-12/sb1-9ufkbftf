# Pinterest kit for Calm Budget Planner

Pinterest is a search engine, so people find pins by typing phrases like "adhd budget" or "budget spreadsheet". It's the best free traffic source for a product like this. It's slow to build, though: expect weeks, not days.

Images are in `listing/pins/` (`pin-1.png` to `pin-4.png`, 1000×1500).
Replace `YOUR-ETSY-LISTING-LINK` with your listing's web address (copy it from the browser once the listing is live).

---

## Setup (10 minutes)

1. Create a free **Pinterest Business** account (pinterest.com/business), using your shop name.
2. Add your Etsy shop link in your profile and write a short bio, e.g. *"Calm, simple budget spreadsheets for busy brains. Excel + Google Sheets."*
3. Create these 3 boards (add a description to each with the keywords in it):
   - **ADHD Budgeting Tips**: "Simple, low-stress budgeting ideas for ADHD and busy brains."
   - **Budget Spreadsheets & Templates**: "Excel and Google Sheets budget templates, bill trackers and expense trackers."
   - **Stop Impulse Spending**: "The 24-hour rule and other ways to curb impulse buys."

## Posting plan (2 weeks)

- **Post 1 pin a day, not all at once.** Pinterest favours steady posting. Pin 1 on day 1, pin 2 on day 2, and so on, then repeat each pin to a second relevant board a week or two later.
- **Mix in helpful pins.** Don't only promote. See the ideas below.
- **Don't overdo hashtags.** Pinterest relies on the words in your title and description more than hashtags. 2 to 4 is plenty.
- **Check Pinterest Analytics after about 2 weeks.** Make more pins in the style of whichever got the most clicks. Pins can take weeks to start getting traffic, so don't judge on day 3.

## Helpful pin ideas (make in Canva or ask me for images)

1. **"The 24-hour rule"**: 4 short steps: write it down, wait a day, still want it? buy it, otherwise skip.
2. **"What does 'safe to spend' mean?"**: (income − bills − savings − everyday spending) ÷ days left this month.
3. **"5 bills people forget"**: annual subscriptions, car registration, insurance, memberships, school fees. Link to the template.
4. **"Why budgets fail when you're busy"**: too many tabs, red warnings, strict daily tracking. A calmer alternative: link to the template.
5. **"Subscription clean-up in 10 minutes"**: list them, note the last time you used each, cancel what you haven't.

## Honest notes

- I can't promise traffic or sales. Pinterest takes time to build, and results vary a lot.
- Always link to your own listing. Don't use pins that promise results you can't back up (like "fix your finances" or income claims).
- Make sure the destination link works before posting.
